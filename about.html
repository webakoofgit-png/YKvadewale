Our Story

From the bustling streets of Mumbai to your neighborhood - discover the passion and tradition behind YK Vadewale

Where Flavor Meets Purpose

At YK Vadewale, we're not just serving food — we're serving memories, tradition, and a mission to bring authentic Mumbai street food to every corner of the city.

🏙️ Born on Mumbai Streets

Our journey began in 2016 when Yogesh Kumar, a passionate food enthusiast, started selling vada pav from a small cart near Dadar station. What started as a humble venture to support his family quickly became a local sensation, with people lining up daily for his perfectly spiced vadas and freshly baked pavs.

Yogesh's secret wasn't just in the recipe - it was in his commitment to using only the freshest ingredients, maintaining strict hygiene standards, and treating every customer like family. Word spread quickly through the neighborhood, and soon people were traveling from across Mumbai just to taste his authentic vada pav.

🌱 Rooted in Tradition, Driven by Quality

Every recipe at YK Vadewale is rooted in authentic Maharashtrian traditions, passed down through generations. We believe that great food does more than satisfy hunger — it sparks nostalgia, builds connections, and tells a story of our rich cultural heritage.

Our menu is 100% vegetarian, preservative-free, and crafted fresh with care. We source our ingredients from local farmers and suppliers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. Every plate reflects our belief that authentic street food should be both delicious and wholesome.

🚀 Growing into a Movement

Today, YK Vadewale has grown from a single cart to 25+ outlets across Mumbai and beyond. But our mission remains the same: to bring the authentic taste of Mumbai's streets to every neighborhood, while maintaining the same quality and care that made us famous.

We're committed to offering more than just a meal — we deliver a vibrant, hygienic, and soul-satisfying experience that honors the legacy of Indian street food. Every new outlet is a step towards making authentic Mumbai flavors accessible to food lovers everywhere.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped YK Vadewale into the beloved brand it is today

2016

The Beginning

Started with a single cart near Dadar station. Yogesh Kumar's passion for authentic street food begins to attract local customers.

2017

First Outlet

Opened our first permanent outlet in Dadar. Introduced seating and expanded menu to include misal pav and bhajiya.

2019

Expansion Begins

Opened 5 more outlets across Mumbai. Received "Best Street Food" award from Mumbai Food Critics Association.

2021

Franchise Launch

Launched franchise program. Opened 10 franchise outlets across Maharashtra. Introduced online ordering and delivery.

2023

Digital Revolution

Launched mobile app and cloud kitchen model. Reached 15,000+ satisfied customers. Featured in major food publications.

2024

Future Ready

25+ outlets operational. Planning expansion to other states. Committed to sustainable practices and community development.
